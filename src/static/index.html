fund-flows API

Flows recorded against a client and fund, summarised by month, and reconciled against the total the sales side reported.

When those two numbers disagree, someone has to find out where. The table below is live: it is the output of GET /reconciliation.

Endpoints

  • GET /clients reference data, by name
  • GET /clients/3/summary month by month, and the gap
  • GET /flows filter with clientId, fundId, month
  • GET /funds reference data
  • GET /reconciliation every disagreement, either direction
  • GET /health liveness
  • POST /flows record a movement
  • PUT /clients/:id/reported-totals record what the business says a month came to

All data is fictional. Source and tests at github.com/MNB4556/fund-flows-api.
